What we license

Three modules. One licence.

Each module is licensed independently or as a stack, and integrates into your existing systems rather than replacing them.

MOD-01 · INGEST

Data Ingestion & Normalisation

Pulls structured and unstructured data from your existing sources and reshapes it into a consistent format the decision engine can act on — no manual reconciliation.

MOD-02 · ENGINE

Decision Engine

A configurable rules-and-model layer that turns normalised data into recommended or automated actions, tuned to thresholds your team defines and controls.

MOD-03 · INTERFACE

Operational Interfaces

Dashboards and APIs that surface every decision the engine makes, with a full audit trail, so your team can review, override, or approve without digging through logs.

How licensing works

From first call to live deployment.

A structured, technical process — built for teams who need to evaluate before they commit.

01

Technical scoping call

We map your existing data sources and decision points against our module set to confirm fit before anything is proposed.

02

Sandbox deployment

A contained environment using your own (or representative) data, so your team can test behaviour before any licence is signed.

03

Licensing agreement

Clear terms covering usage rights, data handling, support levels, and renewal — reviewed by your legal team, not just ours.

04

Rollout & support

Phased deployment into production with an assigned technical contact and scheduled module updates for the life of the licence.

Governance

Your data. Your thresholds. Your override.

Licensing our technology doesn't mean handing over control of your operations. Every deployment is scoped, reviewable, and reversible.

  • Deployed inside your environment Modules run within your infrastructure or a contracted private instance — your data does not need to leave your control to be processed.
  • Configurable thresholds, not black boxes Every automated action is governed by rules your team sets and can adjust — nothing acts outside the boundaries you define.
  • Full audit trail Every decision the engine makes is logged, timestamped, and reviewable — exportable for your own compliance and audit processes.

Specific certifications, regulatory registrations, and data-processing terms are confirmed during the technical scoping call and set out in the licensing agreement.
